# Private third-actor corrected commercial decision (staged only)

This slice adds an immutable **internal commercial decision receipt** for corrected revision-5 scope. It is **not** an authorization to quote, bill, transmit, deploy, or release a revised record. A source stays at revision 5 with `scope_review = NULL`; no legacy reader/writer has a corrected quote path.

The decision function `bm_intake_private.review_corrected_commercial_draft` requires three distinct people: the original creator (current member), the independent correction-evidence reviewer (current owner/operator), and a THIRD current owner/operator who examines the fresh corrected human scope selection. The trusted server must derive the reviewer identity from independently verified current Auth, never a browser-provided actor. SQL locks these membership records against revocation and requires an exact current approved company policy head and version.

It verifies the immutable original, correction hold, candidate edit history, versioned correction request hash, independent evidence acceptance, immutable activation, original-creator renewed scope selection and exact selection receipt. It recomputes source/candidate/draft/evidence/activation/workbench/review digests, replaying edits against the original provider extraction; it separately checks that the selected corrected services remain charged/included and satisfy any optional add-on acknowledgments under the locked active company policy. A changed policy after selection fails closed. No caller-supplied policy JSON or prices are accepted.

The table `bm_intake_private.corrected_commercial_decisions` stores exactly one immutable `approve-internal-draft` or `reject-internal-draft` decision plus bounded human reason and database-produced receipt digest. Browser roles cannot call the private function, and no browser or `service_role` has direct DML to the private table. A rejection cannot be overwritten, and approving an internal draft does **not** authorize a customer quote.

**Hard limits:** This is disposable staged SQL, not integrated live Supabase Auth/RLS/private Storage, not a secure webhook, and not an approved commercial delivery flow. A separate final release contract with actual pricing calculations, tenant-authenticated offer and send approvals, AV/retention and security/privacy/legal sign-off is required. No production tables or direct customer interfaces are enabled. See the mandatory disposable PostgreSQL positive/negative test.
