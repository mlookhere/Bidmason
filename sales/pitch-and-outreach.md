# BidMason Pitch & Outreach Kit (BidMason Commercial Kit — internal sales version)

Never say AI. Sell bigger contracts. The buyer wants jobs, not technology.

## Approved public language

- "We build commercial sales systems for contractors."
- "We help you look more professional when bidding larger jobs."
- "We turn walkthrough notes into polished proposals."
- "We help you compete with bigger companies."
- "We make your quotes look like they came from a national contractor, without losing your local-business personality."

**Banned:** AI, automation buzzwords, SaaS jargon, machine learning, agents, tokens, prompts.

## The core pitch

> "Most contractors are good at the work but lose bigger commercial jobs because their website, quote process, and proposals look too casual. We build you a professional commercial sales system: a better website, quote intake, polished proposal templates, pricing tiers, and follow-up emails so property managers and business owners take you seriously."

Then the closer:

> "Send me one old quote or job walkthrough and I'll turn it into a polished commercial proposal so you can see the difference."

The free sample closes people. Always offer it.

Proof first: send me one old quote or walkthrough note and I'll turn it into a polished commercial proposal, free. If you don't like it, you've lost nothing.

{Name}
BidMason | Commercial sales systems for contractors

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I get enough work from referrals." | "Referrals are the best leads. This is about what happens when that referral is a property manager comparing three bids. The work gets you in the door, and the proposal wins the contract." |
| "I have a website." | "Most contractor sites are built for homeowners. Commercial buyers look for different things: insurance, service programs, and a proposal process. Want me to show you the difference on your own site?" |
| "Too expensive." | "The Growth setup pays for itself with one mid-size commercial account. What's a monthly HOA contract worth to you over a year?" |
| "I don't have time for this." | "That's the point. You send a 2-minute voice note after a walkthrough; a finished proposal comes back. It takes time off your plate, not on." |

## Where to find the first 300

- Google Maps: {trade} companies in target metro with weak/no websites
- HOA/property-management association vendor lists
- Supplier counters (janitorial supply, pressure washer dealers, landscape supply)
- Local contractor Facebook groups and BNI chapters
- Yelp/Thumbtack pros with good reviews and bad web presence (good work, weak paperwork = ideal client)
