# Follow-Up Sequence Master Templates (BidMason Follow-Up)

Seven messages, merge fields in `{braces}`. Customize per client with their actual services, voice, and the prospect's actual property — never send the bare template. Tone is approved once per client at setup.

---

## 1. Thanks for the walkthrough — sends same day

**Subject:** Today's walkthrough at {property name}

{First name},

Thanks for walking me through {property name} today. Quick recap of what we looked at: {2–3 scope items, e.g., "the front walkways, the trash enclosure, and the pool deck"}.

I'll have a full proposal with options over to you by {day}. If anything else comes up you'd like included, just reply here.

{Contractor name}
{Phone}

---

## 2. Proposal delivery — sends with the PDF attached

**Subject:** {Service} proposal for {property name}

{First name},

Attached is our proposal for {property name}. Based on what we saw, I'd recommend the {recommended tier name}. It covers {1-line scope summary} at {frequency} for {price or "the price shown on page 2"}.

There are two other options in there if you want lighter or fuller coverage, and pricing is good for 30 days. Happy to walk through it on a 10-minute call if that's easier.

{Contractor name}

---

## 3. 2-day follow-up — sends 2 days after proposal if no reply

**Subject:** Re: {Service} proposal for {property name}

{First name},

Wanted to make sure the proposal came through okay. If any of the options aren't quite the right fit, I'm glad to adjust the scope or schedule. That's a ten-minute change on our end.

{Contractor name}

---

## 4. 7-day follow-up — sends 7 days after proposal if no reply

**Subject:** Re: {Service} proposal for {property name}

{First name},

One thing I noticed at the walkthrough worth mentioning: {specific observation, e.g., "the gum buildup near the north entrance will take an extra pass on the first visit, so we've priced that in"}.

{Optional, only if true: "Our {month} schedule is starting to fill, so if you'd like service to start this month, this week is the time to lock it in."}

Want me to revise any of the options?

{Contractor name}

---

## 5. Still interested? / Lost-deal close — sends day 14–21 if no reply

**Subject:** {Property name}: should I check back later?

{First name},

I don't want to clutter your inbox. If the timing isn't right on the {service} program, no problem at all. Should I check back next quarter, or close this out for now?

Either way, the walkthrough notes stay on file, so a future quote takes a day, not a week.

{Contractor name}

---

## 6. Won-deal onboarding — sends on acceptance

**Subject:** Welcome aboard: next steps for {property name}

{First name},

Great to have {property/company name} on the schedule. Here's what happens next:

1. You'll receive our certificate of insurance within {n} business days.
2. {Scheduler name/contractor} will confirm your service days for {month}.
3. Our crew lead, {name}, will introduce themselves on the first visit.
4. After the first service, we'll do a quick walkthrough together so the standard is set from day one.

One point of contact for anything: {name, phone, email}.

{Contractor name}

---

## 7. Renewal / upsell — sends ~45 days before term end, or on identified adjacent need

**Subject:** {Property name}: next term and an idea

{First name},

Your current {service} program runs through {date}. Renewal at the same scope is ready whenever you are. Reply "renew" and we'll take care of the rest.

One idea while I have you: we're already on-site {frequency} for {current scope}. Adding {adjacent scope, e.g., "the parking structure"} would run about {price/basis} since the crew's already there, which is meaningfully less than a standalone program.

Worth a look on the next visit?

{Contractor name}

---

## Operator notes
- Trigger conditions assume "no reply"; any reply pauses the sequence and goes to the contractor.
- Incentives (msg 4) and pricing (msg 7) require contractor confirmation before first use.
- Approve one tone variant per client at setup: standard (above) or warmer/more formal variants from the Sales Assistant.
