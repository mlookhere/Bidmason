# First Hire: Client Build & Proposal QA Coordinator

The first hire is not a developer. It's a QC/account coordinator who follows checklists, not vibes. The Inspector's checklists (`agents/08-inspector.md`) are written to be handed to this person unchanged.

## Job summary

Review every client deliverable (websites, proposals, monthly reports) against fixed checklists before the operator sees it. Flag issues, fix the trivial ones, and keep client accounts moving without becoming a bottleneck.

## What they check

- Website links, form submissions, mobile layout
- Business details (name spelling, phone, address, service area — NAP consistency)
- Proposal formatting, pricing fields filled, math correct
- Missing photos / placeholder content
- Repeated AI-sounding phrases ("look no further," "trusted partner," "solutions," "we pride ourselves")
- Final PDF appearance: page breaks, branding, signature page
- Cross-contamination: another client's name anywhere is a fire-the-vendor error — top priority check

## What they do NOT do

- Approve pricing, scope, exclusions, guarantees, or credential claims (operator only)
- Send anything to a client (operator/contractor only)
- Edit core templates

## Pay structure (per-unit, keeps incentives aligned)

| Unit | Rate |
|---|---|
| Website QC (full checklist) | $50–$100 |
| Proposal QC | $10–$25 |
| Monthly client account check | $100–$200 |

Hourly is acceptable early; move to per-unit once volume is steady.

## Daily workflow

1. Open the QC queue (deliverables marked "ready for QC").
2. Run the matching Inspector checklist top to bottom. No skipping; "not checked" is logged, never silently passed.
3. Fix trivial issues directly (typos, dead links); log as "fixed in review."
4. File a QA Report per deliverable: PASS/FAIL, blocking issues, non-blocking, coverage list.
5. Escalate to the operator anything touching the "must QC closely" list (`operations/qc-checklists.md`).

## Hiring filter

Give candidates a deliberately broken sample site and proposal (8 seeded errors: 1 wrong phone number, 1 dead link, 1 `$____` placeholder, 1 other client's name, 1 broken form, 1 credential claim not in the brief, 2 typos). Hire whoever finds 7+ and writes them up clearly.
