## 1. Overview

Thanks for walking me through Mariner's Landing, Dana. With five to six turns a month across the two buildings, the property is a strong fit for our Reserved Turn Program: fixed per-unit pricing, your colors kept on file, and a 48-hour target window from unit access so vacant days don't stack up. The quarterly common-area option below covers the hallways and both laundry rooms we looked at.

## 2. Scope of Work (per unit turn)

| Area | Work performed | Standard of completion |
|---|---|---|
| Walls | Patch nail holes and minor dings (up to 4" repairs), spot-prime, apply finish coat in unit color on file | Uniform finish, no visible patches or holidays under normal light |
| Ceilings | Spot-prime water and scuff marks, apply finish coat | Uniform flat finish, no flashing |
| Trim & baseboards | Caulk gaps, fill nicks, apply enamel finish coat | Smooth enamel finish, clean caulk lines |
| Doors & frames | Clean, scuff-sand, apply enamel finish coat to both faces and frame | Even coverage, hardware free of paint |
| Closeout | Walk unit, remove materials, photo set sent same day | Unit broom-clean, photo confirmation delivered |

Heavy-condition units (smoke, nicotine, heavy staining) require full stain-blocking prime and are billed at the unit rate plus the heavy-condition rate shown in Section 3. We confirm with you by photo before treating any unit as heavy-condition.

Program basis: Reserved and Full Property per-unit rates reflect a 10% volume adjustment from standard rates in exchange for all property turns. At your estimated 5-6 turns per month (typical mix), the Reserved Program runs roughly $2,250-$2,700/month; the Full Property Program adds $475 per quarterly visit.

**Need a different schedule? We'll build a program around it.**

## 4. Service Expectations

- Vacancy notice by phone, text, or email; units scheduled on receipt.
- Certificate of insurance delivered to Pacific Crest before the first unit.
- Uniformed crew, paint stored only in the agreed locked storage room, site left broom-clean.
- Photo set per completed unit, same day.
- One point of contact: Marcus Delgado, (619) 555-0168.
- First-month review walkthrough with you after the first four turns, so the standard is set early.

## 5. Assumptions

Pricing assumes: units are vacant and accessible at the scheduled time; colors and sheens match the property color sheet on file; standard unit condition apart from identified heavy-condition units; functioning lighting and power in units; use of the on-site locked storage room; walk-up access in Building A as walked on June 10.

## 6. Exclusions

Not included: drywall repairs larger than 4 inches (quoted per repair before work); cabinet, tub, or countertop refinishing; mold or moisture damage remediation; pest-related preparation; full common-area repaints; exterior painting; repairs to damage caused by other trades after our completion photos. If the buildings predate 1978, lead-safe (RRP) work practices apply and are confirmed in writing before the first turn.

## 7. Timeline

Program can start within two weeks of acceptance. Service days: Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM. Typical turn: one working day in the unit; heavy-condition units up to two.
