## Positioning statement

## Service hierarchy

1. **Lead (recurring commercial):** Unit-Turn Painting Programs · Common-Area Paint Programs
2. **Supporting:** HOA & multifamily exterior repaints · Elastomeric/waterproof coatings · Office/retail after-hours repaints
3. **Secondary/one-time:** Residential repaints (kept, not promoted) · Deck & fence staining

## Packages (lead service: unit turns)

| | As-Needed Turns | **Reserved Turn Program** (intended choice) | Full Property Program |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commitment | None | All turns for the property | All turns + common areas |
| Scheduling | 3-4 business days | 48-hour target window | 48-hour target window |
| Per-unit price | Standard rates | Volume rate (about 10% under standard) | Volume rate |
| Extras | — | Monthly invoice, color log kept on file | Quarterly common-area touch-up day + annual property paint report |

Pricing computed from brief basics (studio $400 / 1BR $500 / 2BR $600 standard). Custom schedule line on every table.

## Rationale notes for operator

Chosen angle: productized unit turns. The simulated field shows painters treating turns as filler and make-ready bundlers doing them badly; a program with fixed per-unit pricing and a turnaround target is differentiated and recurring. **Rejected alternative 1:** HOA-exterior-first (Bayside owns it in the demo field; project-based, not recurring). **Rejected alternative 2:** general "commercial painting" (undifferentiated against Citywide). Tradeoff to watch: 48-hour target is the offer's spine, so capacity planning matters more than marketing (see Analyst open question 1).
