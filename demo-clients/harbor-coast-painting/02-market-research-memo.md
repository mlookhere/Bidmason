**The gap:** nobody in the simulated field owns "dedicated unit-turn painting program with guaranteed-window turnaround." Make-ready bundlers do it as a sideline with weak paint quality; painting companies treat turns as filler work. A painter who productizes the turn (fixed per-unit pricing, reserved capacity, 48-hour target) has a clear lane.

## Commercial buyer landscape (ranked by recurring potential)

1. **Multifamily property managers** — every vacancy is a paint job; portfolio managers buy programs, not jobs
2. **HOA boards** — repaint cycles every 7-10 years plus continuous common-area touch-up budgets
3. **Office/retail property managers** — after-hours repaints, tenant-improvement touch-ups
4. **Hotels/motels** — room refresh cycles (later target; demanding scheduling)

## Recommended niche angle

> San Diego unit-turn and common-area painting programs for property managers, apartment communities, and HOAs.

## Service demand notes

- **Recurring:** unit turns (lead with this), common-area programs, HOA touch-up retainers
- **Project-based but repeatable:** HOA/multifamily exterior repaints (feed from the program relationships)
- **De-emphasize:** residential repaints (owner wants out; keep one page, no homepage placement)
